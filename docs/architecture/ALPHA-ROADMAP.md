\# JustDefenders Alpha Roadmap



\## Mission



Deliver an Alpha release that provides immediate value to Defender owners by

helping them identify the correct part, understand why it is correct, obtain

it from appropriate suppliers and maintain a continuously evolving Digital

Twin of their vehicle.



The Alpha release is not intended to demonstrate every planned capability.



Its purpose is to validate the core Operational Intelligence philosophy with

real Defender owners.



\---



\# Alpha Success Criteria



A Defender owner should be able to:



✓ Register their Defender.



✓ Build their Digital Twin.



✓ Search for a part using any identifier.



✓ Confirm compatibility.



✓ View related components.



✓ Find suitable Australian suppliers.



✓ Record maintenance.



✓ Capture Evidence.



✓ Record fuel.



✓ Receive meaningful Operational Insights.



✓ Export maintenance and evidence reports.



✓ Use the platform with confidence.



If these objectives are achieved, Alpha has been successful.

\# Alpha Guiding Principle



Every completed sprint should make it easier for a Defender owner to:



Identify



Understand



Procure



Maintain



Document



and preserve the operational history of their Defender.



If a development task does not improve one or more of these outcomes, its

priority should be reconsidered.



\---



\# Definition of Alpha Complete



Alpha is complete when a Defender owner can confidently manage the ongoing

maintenance and operational history of their vehicle using JustDefenders

without needing multiple disconnected applications, spreadsheets or paper

records.



The platform should become the trusted operational companion for Defender

ownership.



Alpha is the beginning of that journey.
