\# Purpose



This Knowledge Register defines the supported Land Rover Defender variants

within the JustDefenders Operational Intelligence Platform.



It represents the authoritative reference for supported production models

during the Alpha and early Beta phases.



All platform components shall reference this register rather than maintaining

independent vehicle lists.



\---



\# Architectural Principle



Vehicle support shall prioritise operational depth rather than breadth.



Every supported variant should receive comprehensive operational knowledge,

parts intelligence and Digital Twin support.

\# Specialist Variants



The platform architecture also supports recognised specialist Defender

variants where operationally appropriate.



Examples include:



\* High Capacity Pick-Up (HCPU)

\* Cab Chassis

\* Dual Cab conversions

\* Utility Wagon

\* Station Wagon

\* County Station Wagon

\* Special Vehicles (approved conversions)



Military variants will be addressed under a separate operational register.



\---



\# Future Expansion



Future support may include additional Land Rover heritage vehicles.



Examples include:



\* Series I

\* Series II

\* Series IIA

\* Series III



Such expansion shall occur only after comprehensive support for Defender

variants has been achieved.



This approach aligns with the platform principle of \*\*Depth Before Breadth\*\*.
