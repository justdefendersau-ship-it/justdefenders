\# Purpose



This Knowledge Register defines the canonical Australian supplier ecosystem

supported by the JustDefenders Operational Intelligence Platform.



The register provides the authoritative reference for supplier identity,

specialisation, procurement intelligence and commercial integration.



Supplier performance, operational reliability and customer experience are

evaluated separately through Operational Intelligence and are not defined

within this register.



\---



\# Architectural Principle



This register records supplier facts.



Operational Intelligence evaluates supplier performance.



The two responsibilities shall remain separate.



\---



\# Supplier Categories



Suppliers may operate within one or more specialist categories including:



\* Genuine Land Rover Parts

\* OEM Components

\* Aftermarket Components

\* Performance Upgrades

\* Touring Equipment

\* Recovery Equipment

\* Electrical

\* Driveline

\* Suspension

\* Cooling

\* Steering

\* Braking

\* Engine Components

\* Body Components

\* Interior Components

\* Accessories

\* Used Parts

\* Reconditioned Components

\* Workshop Services



\---



\# Canonical Supplier Record



Each supplier record should ultimately contain:



\* Business name

\* Trading name

\* Location

\* Website

\* Contact information

\* Operating regions

\* Product categories

\* Supported Defender variants

\* OEM partnerships

\* Known brands supplied

\* Delivery capabilities

\* Commercial partner status

\* API integration status



This register intentionally excludes subjective performance assessments.



\---



\# Procurement Philosophy



The objective of JustDefenders is not to recommend the cheapest supplier.



The objective is to assist owners in selecting the most appropriate supplier

for the operational requirement.



Recommendations may consider:



\* Availability

\* Compatibility

\* Operational suitability

\* Geographic location

\* Delivery requirements

\* Expedition urgency

\* Warranty availability

\* Supplier capability



Supplier recommendations should always remain explainable.
