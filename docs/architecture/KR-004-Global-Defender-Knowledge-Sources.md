\# Purpose



This Knowledge Register defines the authoritative external knowledge sources

used by the JustDefenders Knowledge Acquisition Engine.



The purpose of this register is to identify trusted sources of Defender

knowledge that may contribute to Operational Intelligence, while ensuring that

all acquired information is validated before influencing platform

recommendations.



\---



\# Architectural Principle



External information is an input to Operational Intelligence.



It is never Operational Intelligence by itself.



Knowledge must always be evaluated within the context of the owner's Digital

Twin, Operational History and supporting Evidence.



\---



\# Categories of Knowledge Sources



The platform may acquire knowledge from:



\* Defender owner forums

\* Land Rover clubs

\* Technical publications

\* Workshop manuals

\* Parts catalogues

\* Technical Service Bulletins

\* Supplier technical documentation

\* Restoration journals

\* Expedition reports

\* Engineering articles

\* Manufacturer publications

\* Community-maintained knowledge repositories



\---



\# Knowledge Validation



Knowledge obtained from external sources shall be evaluated before influencing

Operational Intelligence.



Validation may consider:



\* Multiple independent sources

\* Technical consistency

\* Supporting Evidence

\* Historical accuracy

\* Specialist review

\* Community consensus

\* Operational outcomes



Popularity alone shall never determine correctness.



\---



\# Knowledge Classification



Acquired knowledge should be classified wherever possible by:



\* Supported Defender variant

\* Engine

\* Transmission

\* Transfer case

\* Axle

\* Model year

\* System (Cooling, Fuel, Electrical, etc.)

\* Expedition relevance

\* Operational environment

\* Confidence level



This enables precise retrieval and improves the quality of Operational

Intelligence.
