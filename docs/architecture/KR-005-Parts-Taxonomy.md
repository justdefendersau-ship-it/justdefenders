\# Purpose



This Knowledge Register defines the canonical classification system for all

parts managed by the JustDefenders Operational Intelligence Platform.



The taxonomy provides a consistent structure for Parts Intelligence,

Procurement Intelligence, Supplier Intelligence, Operational Insights and the

Digital Twin.



Every part within the platform shall be classified using this taxonomy.



\---



\# Architectural Principle



A part should exist only once within the platform.



It may participate in many Operational Intelligence workflows, but its

classification shall always remain canonical.



\---
