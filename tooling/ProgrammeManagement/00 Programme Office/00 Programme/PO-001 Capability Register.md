\## Purpose



The Capability Register is the authoritative inventory of all JustDefenders platform capabilities.



Every engineering work package shall contribute to one or more registered capabilities.



Capabilities progress through the following lifecycle:



• Design



• Prototype



• Partial



• Commissioning



• Operational



• Production



No engineering work package shall exist without traceability to this register.
