\# Alpha Definition



A JustDefenders Alpha release is achieved when a member can use both the web application and the mobile application to:



• Authenticate securely.



• Register one or more Defender vehicles.



• Maintain their Garage.



• Search for compatible parts using VIN, model or part number.



• Understand why a recommended part is compatible.



• Access supporting technical knowledge.



• Compare supplier pricing and availability.



• Complete an approved procurement workflow.



• Record the installation within their vehicle history.



Both clients shall operate against the same operational platform, services, intelligence and data.
