\# PO-007 — Programme Office Rationalisation



\*\*Document ID:\*\* PO-007  

\*\*Title:\*\* Programme Office Rationalisation  

\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0  

\*\*Status:\*\* Controlled  

\*\*Date:\*\* 7th August 2026  

\*\*Owner:\*\* Programme Office



\---



\# Purpose



This document defines the engineering strategy for establishing a single authoritative Programme Office.



The objective is to eliminate duplicated documentation, conflicting structures, inconsistent numbering and manual administration prior to Alpha commissioning.



This document governs the rationalisation of the Programme Office only.



It does not modify engineering work packages or production source code.



\---



\# Objectives



The Programme Office shall provide:



\- one authoritative folder structure

\- one authoritative document structure

\- one authoritative numbering system

\- one authoritative engineering history

\- one authoritative milestone history

\- automated validation

\- automated index generation

\- automated cross-reference generation



\---



\# Scope



This rationalisation includes:



\- Programme Office

\- Programme documentation

\- Engineering Work Packages

\- Milestones

\- Strategy

\- Governance

\- Planning

\- Architecture

\- Engineering

\- Delivery

\- Quality

\- Security



\---



\# Principles



The following principles shall govern all Programme Office activities.



\## PO-007-001



Nothing shall be deleted during rationalisation.



\---



\## PO-007-002



Documents shall be archived rather than removed.



\---



\## PO-007-003



Every controlled document shall have a unique document identifier.



\---



\## PO-007-004



Every controlled document shall have one authoritative location.



\---



\## PO-007-005



Folder structures shall remain stable.



\---



\## PO-007-006



Automation shall replace repetitive manual administration wherever practical.



\---



\# Work Packages



The following engineering work packages are authorised.



\## Phase 1



Programme Office Audit



Inventory every folder.



Inventory every document.



Inventory every numbering sequence.



\---



\## Phase 2



Programme Office Rationalisation



Identify:



\- duplicates

\- obsolete documents

\- superseded documents

\- orphan documents

\- inconsistent numbering



\---



\## Phase 3



Engineering Work Package Structure



Populate the Engineering Work Packages hierarchy.



Associate every PR with its parent milestone.



\---



\## Phase 4



Metadata Standardisation



Introduce a common metadata format for every controlled document.



\---



\## Phase 5



Cross Reference Standardisation



Establish explicit relationships between:



\- Programme documents

\- Milestones

\- Engineering work packages

\- Architecture

\- Governance



\---



\## Phase 6



Programme Office Automation



Implement automated Programme Office maintenance.



\---



\# Deliverables



The Programme Office shall ultimately provide:



\- automated Programme Office Index

\- automated Capability Register updates

\- automated Roadmap updates

\- automated Milestone updates

\- automated numbering validation

\- automated duplicate detection

\- automated cross-reference validation

\- Programme Office health reporting



\---



\# Success Criteria



Programme Office Rationalisation is complete when:



\- every controlled document has one authoritative location

\- duplicate document purposes have been removed

\- numbering is internally consistent

\- Engineering Work Packages have an authoritative home

\- Programme Office automation can maintain the document set with minimal manual intervention



\---



\# Engineering Objective



Establish a trustworthy Programme Office capable of supporting Alpha engineering without ambiguity or duplicated administration.
