# Purpose

The Programme Office is the governing authority for the JustDefenders Engineering Programme.

It provides the programme structure, engineering governance, work package control, architectural direction and milestone management for all engineering activities.

All engineering work shall be traceable through the Programme Office.

---

# Programme Vision

JustDefenders is an operational intelligence platform for Land Rover Defender owners.

The programme objective is to deliver an integrated web and mobile platform enabling members to:

• Manage their Defender vehicles.

• Discover compatible parts.

• Access technical knowledge.

• Compare suppliers.

• Record maintenance.

• Build long-term operational intelligence.

# Engineering Principles

The programme follows these principles.

1.

Commission before creating.

Existing capabilities shall be identified and integrated before new functionality is introduced.

2.

Operational capability takes precedence over architectural expansion.

3.

Every capability shall belong to a registered platform.

4.

Every engineering activity shall be traceable.

5.

Both the Web Platform and the Mobile Platform are first-class clients.

Neither platform is subordinate to the other.

6.

Operational Intelligence is the foundation of the platform.

Supplier Intelligence, Parts Intelligence, Knowledge Intelligence and Vehicle Intelligence build upon this operational foundation.
