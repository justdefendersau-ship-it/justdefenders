WP-BUILD-009B PATCH

Replace the naive assembly loop with a statement-aware normalizer:

Algorithm:
1. Split each revision into lines.
2. Maintain two states:
   - Normal
   - SkipExportBlock
3. In Normal:
   - Collect and de-duplicate lines matching: ^\s*using\s+namespace\s+
   - On ^\s*Export-ModuleMember\b switch to SkipExportBlock.
   - Emit all other lines.
4. In SkipExportBlock:
   - Continue skipping while the previous logical statement continues
     (lines ending with PowerShell continuation backtick).
   - Return to Normal after the statement terminates.
5. Before writing Engineering-Builder.psm1:
   - Emit all unique using namespace directives once at the top.
6. After all revision bodies:
   - Emit a single consolidated Export-ModuleMember block.

This patch preserves all other bootstrap behaviour.
