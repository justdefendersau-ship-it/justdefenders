\# Engineering Handover Pack



\## Overview



The Engineering Handover Pack is the authoritative engineering knowledge base

for the JustDefenders platform.



It preserves the engineering architecture, development methodology,

engineering standards, platform recovery history, architectural decisions,

rediscovered project knowledge, work package planning and Alpha roadmap.



The Handover Pack forms part of the official JustDefenders project artefacts

and is maintained with the same engineering discipline as the source code.

\# Engineering Standards



The Engineering Handover Pack follows these principles.



\- Documentation is treated as source code.

\- Engineering knowledge is protected intellectual property.

\- Historical information is never intentionally discarded.

\- Every engineering session concludes with documentation updates.

\- Every major engineering decision is recorded.

\- Documentation versions align with engineering checkpoints.

\- Shared engineering modules are developed before applications.

\- Whole-file development is preferred over incremental patching.

\# Engineering Philosophy



The Engineering Handover Pack exists to ensure that no engineering knowledge,

architectural rationale or project intellectual property is lost.



The documentation shall remain aligned with the source code and be maintained

throughout the lifetime of the JustDefenders platform.



It provides engineering continuity between development sessions, engineers and

AI assistants, ensuring that the platform continues to evolve on a stable,

well-documented and professionally governed engineering foundation.
