JDEP Engineering Principles



1\.

One Engineering Unit = One Complete Production File



2\.

Every Engineering Unit is committed immediately.



3\.

Generated artefacts shall not be edited manually.



4\.

Engineers maintain source.

JDEP maintains metadata.



5\.

Every repetitive engineering activity becomes a JDEP command.



6\.

Git is the engineering history.



7\.

Engineering discussions never become engineering artefacts.



8\.

Production files are the only source of truth.



9\.

Engineering Platforms build Engineering Platforms.



10\.

JDEP must eventually be capable of building itself.
