\# Purpose



This document records the engineering surface rationalisation programme for the JustDefenders engineering platform.



The objective is to improve engineering clarity, reduce structural entropy and establish clear ownership boundaries without altering production behaviour.



This work package is evidence-based and derives its findings from the Engineering Platform Capability Audit.



\---



\# Objectives



\- Clarify engineering implementation surfaces.

\- Eliminate ambiguity.

\- Preserve production behaviour.

\- Preserve engineering history.

\- Reduce repository entropy.

\- Prepare the engineering platform for Alpha commissioning.



\---



\# Principles



\- No production behaviour changes.

\- No deletion of engineering history.

\- Recovery artefacts shall be archived, not discarded.

\- Every engineering surface shall have a single clearly defined responsibility.

\- Rationalisation precedes automation.



\---



\# Engineering Surfaces



The following implementation surfaces shall be reviewed.



\- Common

\- Diagnostics

\- Repository

\- Services

\- Tests

\- WorkPackages

\- Documentation



For each surface the review shall determine:



\- Purpose

\- Current Responsibilities

\- Production Status

\- Commissioning Status

\- Rationalisation Opportunities



\---



\# Success Criteria



Engineering Surface Rationalisation is complete when:



\- every engineering surface has a clearly documented purpose

\- overlapping responsibilities have been identified

\- repository entropy has been reduced

\- production behaviour remains unchanged

\- the engineering platform is ready for Alpha commissioning
